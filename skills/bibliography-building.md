## Capability

Takes the bibliographic details captured while reading and note-taking, and formats them into a
bibliography in the citation style the project requires. Orders entries by author surname, applies
the style's field requirements consistently, and flags entries whose details are incomplete rather
than inventing them.

## What It Does

- formats each source according to the requested citation style
- arranges entries alphabetically by author surname
- flags sources with missing information that a person needs to complete
- adds a short relevance note per source when an annotated bibliography is wanted

## When to Use

- once sources have been evaluated and read, and their details captured
- whenever a reference list or annotated bibliography is part of the deliverable

## Inputs

The structured notes gathered during note-taking, and the citation style to apply.

## Outputs

A formatted bibliography in the requested style, with incomplete entries flagged.
